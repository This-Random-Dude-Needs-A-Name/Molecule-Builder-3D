Element pool

Click an element to place an atom. Metals bond ionically (electron transfer). Noble gases are inert — Kr, Xe and Rn react only with F or O.

Click an element in the pool to place your first atom.
Drag atoms together to bond them — green ghost means a legal bond, red means forbidden.
Click a bond to raise its order; Shift+click lowers it.
Double-click two or more molecules to set up a reaction, then pick a condition.
Move around with WASD / arrow keys (Q/E = down/up) or the touch joystick.
